# Word families: what a tutor should check

The word families (Practice › Word family, and the daily puzzle Today's family) were written and then checked by two
independent model review passes and a re-review (`reviewedBy: model-2pass`), not by a native speaker. This list is
the part where a model is most likely to be wrong, and where a mistake would cost the most. A native teacher should
read it once. Quote the form id (in brackets) with a correction.

1. **Not a German word.** The game says "Not a German word" only for these. Each had no DWDS dictionary entry, at most
   10 hits in the DWDS corpora and no wordfreq score, and both review passes judged it not German. If any is used
   anywhere (regional, technical, dated, jocular), it must move to the second list.
2. **Checked and kept as possibly real.** Combinations a reviewer or the lexicon check was not sure about. The game
   never calls these wrong ("not in this family's list").
3. **Words to learn (grade O).** The parts do not give the meaning; check that the meaning and the "why" line are right.
4. **Tricky gender.** Nouns whose article is not what their ending suggests, and participle nouns.

## 3. Words to learn (grade O)

- [bestellen.verb] **bestellen**: order (food, goods). Why: Learn it as a word.
- [der_Verlag] **der Verlag**: publisher, publishing house. Why: Learn it as a word: verlegen also means to publish books, and der Verlag is the firm that does it.
- [ersetzen.verb] **ersetzen**: replace. Why: Learn it as a word: er- does not mean "instead". The new part is set where the old one was.
- [unternehmen.verb] **unternehmen**: do (something), undertake. Why: Learn it as a word. Das Unternehmen = the company.
- [sich_benehmen.verb] **benehmen**: sich benehmen: behave. Why: Learn it as a word.
- [angenehm.adj] **angenehm**: pleasant, agreeable. Why: Learn it as a word: an old adjective (compare genehm, acceptable) for what you are glad to take.
- [sich_ergeben.verb] **ergeben**: yield, show, come to (Die Umfrage ergab …); sich ergeben: arise, follow (from); surrender. Why: Learn it as a word. Das Ergebnis = the result.
- [sich_begeben.verb] **begeben**: sich begeben: go, proceed (formal). Why: Learn it as a word: a formal verb for going somewhere, heard in announcements.
- [umkommen.verb] **umkommen**: die (in an accident or disaster). Why: Learn it as a word: um here does not mean around.
- [bekommen.verb] **bekommen**: get, receive. Why: Learn it as a word. It does not mean "become" (that is werden). A be- verb with an object: hat bekommen.
- [die_Auskunft] **die Auskunft**: information (given when you ask); information desk. Why: Learn it as a word: -kunft is an old noun of kommen; it is not a noun of auskommen (get by).
- [das_Abkommen] **das Abkommen**: agreement, treaty. Why: Learn it as a word: it does not come from vom Weg abkommen (stray).
- [begehen.verb] **begehen**: commit (a mistake, a crime). Why: Learn it as a word.
- [sich_beziehen.verb] **beziehen**: sich beziehen auf: refer to; receive (Gehalt, Rente beziehen); das Bett beziehen: put sheets on. Why: Learn it as a word. In a letter: Ich beziehe mich auf Ihre Anzeige.
- [beziehungsweise.conj] **beziehungsweise**: or rather; respectively (written bzw.). Why: Learn it as a word: Beziehung + -s- + weise; mostly written bzw.
- [gefallen.verb] **gefallen**: please: Das gefällt mir = I like it. Why: Learn it as a word. Its participle is the same as fallen's, but it takes haben: hat gefallen.
- [allenfalls.adv] **allenfalls**: at most; at best, if anything. Why: Learn it as a word: allen Falls (in all cases) has come to mean at most.
- [der_Beifall] **der Beifall**: applause; approval (also der Applaus). Why: Learn it as a word. The old picture: you 'fall in beside' someone, i.e. take their side and approve; the applause sense came later.
- [sich_verhalten.verb] **verhalten**: sich verhalten: behave. Why: Learn it as a word.
- [erhalten.verb] **erhalten**: receive; preserve. Why: Learn it as a word. Formal for bekommen.
- [enthalten.verb] **enthalten**: contain. Why: Learn it as a word.
- [sich_unterhalten.verb] **unterhalten**: sich unterhalten: chat, talk; unterhalten: entertain; support (financially), maintain. Why: Learn it as a word.
- [das_Gehalt] **das Gehalt**: salary. Why: Learn it as a word: ge- + halt once meant what keeps you, your upkeep, and now your pay.
- [versprechen.verb] **versprechen**: promise; sich versprechen: misspeak. Why: Learn "promise" as a word. sich versprechen (misspeak) is ver- = wrong, like sich verschreiben and sich verhören.
- [entsprechen.verb] **entsprechen**: correspond to, match. Why: Learn it as a word.
- [das_Verfahren] **das Verfahren**: procedure, process; (legal) proceedings. Why: Learn it as a word: it comes from verfahren (to proceed, to deal with), not from sich verfahren (get lost).
- [verstehen.verb] **verstehen**: understand. Why: Learn it as a word.
- [gestehen.verb] **gestehen**: confess. Why: Learn it as a word. Hat gestanden can mean "stood" or "confessed".
- [zuständig.adj] **zuständig**: responsible (in charge); competent (an office). Why: Learn it as a word: it comes from zustehen (be due to someone), not from the sense of der Zustand.
- [einverstanden.adj] **einverstanden**: agreed; in agreement (mit: with). Why: Learn it as a word: the participle of an old verb sich einverstehen (come to an understanding).
- [gebieten.verb] **gebieten**: call for, require (formal); command. Why: Learn it as a word: an old verb for command; today mostly 'the situation calls for it'.
- [erbrechen.verb] **erbrechen**: vomit, be sick (formal, medical; also sich erbrechen). Why: Learn it as a word: the formal word for being sick; er- adds no clear picture.
- [das_Gebrechen] **das Gebrechen**: infirmity, ailment (of old age). Why: Learn it as a word: from an old verb gebrechen (to be lacking).
- [gebrechlich.adj] **gebrechlich**: frail, infirm. Why: Learn it as a word: from an old verb gebrechen (to be lacking).
- [umbringen.verb] **umbringen**: kill, murder. Why: Learn it as a word: um- does not give the meaning here; it simply means to kill.
- [der_Befund] **der Befund**: findings, result (of a medical test). Why: Learn it as a word: what a doctor finds after an examination; befinden also means to judge, decide (formal: etwas für gut befinden).
- [empfinden.verb] **empfinden**: feel (an emotion, pain); perceive (as). Why: Learn it as a word: emp- (an old form of ent-) does not give the meaning; think of finding a feeling in yourself.
- [aufhören.verb] **aufhören**: stop, quit. Why: Learn it as a word: auf + hören once meant to stop and listen up; today it just means stop.
- [gehören.verb] **gehören**: belong (to someone); be part of (zu). Why: Learn it as a word: ge- + hear once meant to listen to, obey; now it means to belong.
- [der_Anlass] **der Anlass**: occasion; reason, cause. Why: Learn it as a word: the noun no longer has the sense of anlassen.
- [unterlassen.verb] **unterlassen**: refrain from, stop (doing); fail to do. Why: Learn it as a word: you let an action not happen.
- [unerlässlich.adj] **unerlässlich**: essential, indispensable. Why: Learn it as a word: what cannot be let off (erlassen) is indispensable.
- [lässig.adj] **lässig**: casual, cool, relaxed (style, manner). Why: Learn it as a word: related to lassen, things let go easily.
- [vorläufig.adj] **vorläufig**: provisional, temporary; for the time being. Why: Learn it as a word: vor = ahead; what runs ahead of the final version.
- [geläufig.adj] **geläufig**: familiar, in common use (a word, a name); fluent. Why: Learn it as a word: what runs easily off the tongue.
- [blaumachen.verb] **blaumachen**: skip work or school (colloquial). Why: Learn it as a word: blau (blue) does not explain it.
- [das_Gericht] **das Gericht**: court (of law); dish (a meal). Why: Learn it as a word: a court judges (richten = judge); a dish is food made ready (an older sense of richten).
- [die_Nachricht] **die Nachricht**: message; piece of news. Why: Learn it as a word: once what you go by (sich nach etwas richten); today a message.
- [die_Nachrichten] **die Nachrichten**: the news (on TV, radio); messages. Why: Learn it as a word: the plural of Nachricht is also the news programme.
- [unterrichten.verb] **unterrichten**: teach (a subject); inform (formal). Why: Learn it as a word: unterrichten = teach; formally also inform.
- [entrichten.verb] **entrichten**: pay (a fee, a tax; formal). Why: Learn it as a word: official language for paying a fee or a tax.
- [verrichten.verb] **verrichten**: carry out, perform (work, a task). Why: Learn it as a word: verrichten = do, carry out (work), mostly written.
- [die_Vorrichtung] **die Vorrichtung**: device, mechanism, fixture. Why: Learn it as a word: a device, something set up for a purpose.
- [versagen.verb] **versagen**: fail (stop working, not cope); deny (formal). Why: Learn it as a word: the old sense 'say no, refuse' became 'fail'.
- [untersagen.verb] **untersagen**: prohibit, forbid (officially). Why: Learn it as a word: unter- + say does not give 'forbid'.
- [verschieden.adj] **verschieden**: different; various. Why: Learn it as a word: once the Partizip II of verscheiden (to part, pass away), today simply 'different'.
- [der_Bescheid] **der Bescheid**: Bescheid sagen = let someone know; Bescheid wissen = know (about it); (official) notice, decision. Why: Learn it as a word: from bescheiden (to inform, decide), the answer you are given; Bescheid sagen = let someone know.
- [bescheiden.adj] **bescheiden**: modest; humble. Why: Learn it as a word: once the participle of bescheiden (to allot), so content with what you are given.
- [der_Schiedsrichter] **der Schiedsrichter**: referee; arbitrator. Why: Learn it as a word: Schied is an old word for a settled dispute; the Richter (judge) who settles it.
- [der_Zuschlag] **der Zuschlag**: surcharge, extra charge; extra pay (night work); award (at an auction). Why: Learn it as a word: once the hammer stroke at an auction; now an amount added on.
- [sich_entschließen.verb] **entschließen**: sich entschließen: decide, make up your mind. Why: Learn it as a word: ent- + close does not explain it; you settle a choice for yourself.
- [bestimmen.verb] **bestimmen**: decide, determine; define. Why: Learn it as a word: bestimmen = decide, fix what something is to be.
- [versuchen.verb] **versuchen**: try, attempt; (regional, dated) taste (food). Why: Learn it as a word: ver- adds no clear picture here; it once meant to put something to the test.
- [das_Gesuch] **das Gesuch**: formal request, application (to an office). Why: Learn it as a word: the ge- noun of suchen, a request in writing.
- [erteilen.verb] **erteilen**: give, grant (permission, information, a lesson). Why: Learn it as a word: erteilen = give officially, in fixed phrases (eine Erlaubnis, Auskunft erteilen).
- [betragen.verb] **betragen**: amount to, come to (a sum). Why: Learn it as a word: be- + carry does not give the meaning 'amount to'.
- [angewiesen.adj] **angewiesen**: angewiesen sein auf: depend on, rely on. Why: Learn it as a word: auf etwas angewiesen sein = depend on it.

## 4. Tricky gender

- [die_Angestellte] **die Angestellte** (Angestellte): angestellt (employed) used as a noun: der Angestellte, die Angestellte, ein Angestellter.
- [das_Gesetz] **das Gesetz** (GesEtz): das Gesetz: many ge- nouns are das (das Gesetz, das Gebäude), but not all (der Geruch, der Gewinn, der Gedanke).
- [der_Vorgesetzte] **der Vorgesetzte** (VOrgesetzte): vorgesetzt used as a noun: der Vorgesetzte, die Vorgesetzte, ein Vorgesetzter.
- [der_Nachkomme] **der Nachkomme** (NAchkomme): der Nachkomme: a person, so der, not die like most -e nouns (des Nachkommen).
- [der_Gefallen] **der Gefallen** (GefAllen): der Gefallen (a favour) is masculine, against the das rule of a verb noun; das Gefallen means pleasure (Gefallen an etwas finden).
- [das_Gefälle] **das Gefälle** (GefÄlle): das Gefälle: ge- ... -e collective nouns are das.
- [das_Gehalt] **das Gehalt** (GehAlt): das Gehalt (salary), against the der rule of a bare stem; der Gehalt means content (der Alkoholgehalt).
- [das_Gespräch] **das Gespräch** (GesprÄch): das Gespräch: neuter, against the der rule of a bare stem, like many ge- nouns (but der Geruch): learn the article with the word.
- [das_Geständnis] **das Geständnis** (GestÄndnis): The stem changes: gestand → Geständ-.
- [die_Arbeit] **die Arbeit** (Arbeit): die Arbeit: feminine, an exception to the bare-stem rule (the noun is older than the verb).
- [die_Mitarbeit] **die Mitarbeit** (MItarbeit): die Mitarbeit: feminine like die Arbeit.
- [die_Zusammenarbeit] **die Zusammenarbeit** (ZusAmmenarbeit): die Zusammenarbeit: feminine like die Arbeit.
- [das_Angebot] **das Angebot** (Angebot): das: an + ge + bot; nouns built with ge- are mostly das (das Gebot, das Aufgebot).
- [das_Sonderangebot] **das Sonderangebot** (SOnderangebot): das Sonderangebot, like das Angebot.
- [das_Verbot] **das Verbot** (VerbOt): das, like das Angebot.
- [das_Gebiet] **das Gebiet** (GebIet): das Gebiet: ge- + biet; nouns built with ge- are mostly das (das Gebot, das Gebäude).
- [das_Fachgebiet] **das Fachgebiet** (FAchgebiet): das Fachgebiet, like das Gebiet.
- [das_Naturschutzgebiet] **das Naturschutzgebiet** (NatUrschutzgebiet): das Naturschutzgebiet, like das Gebiet. Stress on -tur-, as in Natur.
- [das_Gebot] **das Gebot** (GebOt): das Gebot: ge- + bot, and ge- nouns are mostly das (like das Gebiet, das Angebot). Not on a board: it is built from the same tiles as das Gebiet.
- [das_Aufgebot] **das Aufgebot** (Aufgebot): das Aufgebot: auf + ge + bot; the -ge- makes it das, like das Angebot.
- [das_Gebrechen] **das Gebrechen** (GebrEchen)
- [das_Fundbüro] **das Fundbüro** (FUndbüro): das Fundbüro: a compound takes the article of its last part, das Büro.
- [der_Fragebogen] **der Fragebogen** (FrAgebogen): der Fragebogen: the last part, der Bogen, gives the article.
- [das_Fragezeichen] **das Fragezeichen** (FrAgezeichen): das Fragezeichen: the last part, das Zeichen, gives the article.
- [das_Gehör] **das Gehör** (GehÖr): das Gehör: neuter, like many ge- nouns (but der Geruch, der Gewinn): learn the article with the word.
- [der_Angehörige] **der Angehörige** (Angehörige): angehörig used as a noun: der Angehörige, die Angehörige, ein Angehöriger; mostly plural: die Angehörigen.
- [das_Verhör] **das Verhör** (VerhÖr): das Verhör: an exception, most bare-stem nouns are der.
- [das_Kaufhaus] **das Kaufhaus** (KAufhaus): das Kaufhaus: a compound takes the gender of its last part, das Haus.
- [die_Kaufkraft] **die Kaufkraft** (KAufkraft): die Kaufkraft: a compound takes the gender of its last part, die Kraft.
- [die_Laufbahn] **die Laufbahn** (LAufbahn): die Laufbahn: a compound takes the article of its last part, die Bahn.
- [der_Leiter] **der Leiter** (LEiter): die Leiter (ladder) is a different word with its own article.
- [das_Leitungswasser] **das Leitungswasser** (LEitungswasser): das Leitungswasser: a compound takes the article of its last part (das Wasser).
- [das_Gericht] **das Gericht** (GerIcht): das Gericht: neuter, like many ge- nouns (but der Geruch). Two meanings: court and dish.
- [das_Hauptgericht] **das Hauptgericht** (HAuptgericht): das Hauptgericht: neuter like das Gericht.
- [die_Berufsschule] **die Berufsschule** (BerUfsschule): die Berufsschule: a compound takes the article of its last part, die Schule.
- [die_Schlagzeile] **die Schlagzeile** (SchlAgzeile): die Schlagzeile: a compound takes the article of its last part, die Zeile.
- [die_Schlussfolgerung] **die Schlussfolgerung** (SchlUssfolgerung): die Schlussfolgerung: a compound takes the gender of its last part, die Folgerung.
- [das_Schloss] **das Schloss** (SchlOss): das Schloss: a bare-stem noun but neuter. It shares its tiles with der Schluss, so only der Schluss goes on a board.
- [das_Gesuch] **das Gesuch** (GesUch): das Gesuch: neuter, unlike der Besuch and der Versuch. Learn the article with the word.
- [das_Gegenteil] **das Gegenteil** (GEgenteil): das Gegenteil: neuter, unlike der Teil.
- [die_Teilzeit] **die Teilzeit** (TEilzeit): die Teilzeit: the last part, die Zeit, gives the article.
- [das_Abteil] **das Abteil** (AbtEil): das Abteil: neuter, an exception to the bare-stem rule. Stressed on the second syllable: Abteil.
- [die_Eintrittskarte] **die Eintrittskarte** (Eintrittskarte): die Eintrittskarte: a compound takes the article of its last part (die Karte).
- [die_Zahl] **die Zahl** (ZAhl): die Zahl: a bare-stem noun but feminine; historically the verb zahlen comes from die Zahl.
- [die_Anzahl] **die Anzahl** (Anzahl): die Anzahl: feminine like die Zahl.
- [die_Postleitzahl] **die Postleitzahl** (POstleitzahl): die Postleitzahl: a compound takes the gender of its last part, die Zahl.
- [die_Geheimzahl] **die Geheimzahl** (GehEimzahl): die Geheimzahl: a compound takes the gender of its last part, die Zahl. geheim is stressed on its second syllable: ge-HEIM-zahl.
- [der_Zahler] **der Zahler** (ZAhler): Not to be confused with der Zähler (meter, counter; numerator).

## 5. New forms (round 7, second pass)

Standard words moved from the "possibly real" list to forms, with a meaning, a clue and an example. Check the
meaning, the helper (aux) and the clue (it must lead to this word, not another of the family).

- [hingehen.verb] **hingehen** (gehen): go there (to a place, an event). Clue: to go there (to an event, a place). Helper: ist hingegangen.
- [ergehen.verb] **ergehen** (gehen): fare, get on (impersonal: es ergeht jemandem gut, schlecht; Wie ist es dir ergangen?); be issued (an order, a ruling). Clue: to fare, to get on (how have you been?). Helper: ist ergangen.
- [mithalten.verb] **mithalten** (halten): keep up (with others, in a race, a discussion). Clue: to keep pace (with the others). Helper: hat mitgehalten.
- [anstehen.verb] **anstehen** (stehen): be due, be coming up (a task, a decision); queue (stand in line). Clue: to be coming up, to be due (a task, a decision). Helper: hat angestanden.
- [zustehen.verb] **zustehen** (stehen): be entitled to, be due to (someone): Das steht dir zu.. Clue: to be someone's by right (holiday, money). Helper: hat zugestanden.
- [vorgeben.verb] **vorgeben** (geben): pretend, claim (falsely); set, lay down (a target, a framework). Clue: to pretend (to be ill); to set (a target). Helper: hat vorgegeben.
- [freigeben.verb] **freigeben** (geben): release, clear (for use); open (a road); approve (a document). Clue: to release, to clear (a road, a file) for use. Helper: hat freigegeben.
- [mitgeben.verb] **mitgeben** (geben): give (someone) something to take along. Clue: to give someone something to take along. Helper: hat mitgegeben.
- [die_Vergebung] **die Vergebung** (geben): forgiveness. Clue: the forgiveness (of a wrong).
- [die_Eintragung] **die Eintragung** (tragen): registration, entry (in an official register). Clue: the registration (in an official register).
- [einträglich.adj] **einträglich** (tragen): lucrative, profitable. Clue: lucrative, profitable (a business).
- [sich_beteiligen.verb] **sich beteiligen** (teilen): sich beteiligen an: take part in, contribute to; jemanden beteiligen: give someone a share. Clue: to take part, to chip in (sich, an). Helper: hat beteiligt.
- [die_Beteiligung] **die Beteiligung** (teilen): participation, involvement; turnout (an election); stake (in a firm). Clue: the participation; the turnout (in a vote).
- [unterziehen.verb] **unterziehen** (ziehen): sich einer Sache unterziehen: undergo (an operation, a test); etwas/jemanden einer Sache unterziehen: subject (something, someone) to (a check, a review). Clue: to undergo (an operation, a test; sich). Helper: hat unterzogen.
- [sich_belaufen.verb] **sich belaufen** (laufen): sich belaufen auf: amount to, come to (a sum). Clue: to amount to (costs, damage; sich, auf). Helper: hat belaufen.
- [hinsehen.verb] **hinsehen** (sehen): look (at it), look closely (instead of looking away). Clue: to look closely (instead of looking away). Helper: hat hingesehen.
- [zuschreiben.verb] **zuschreiben** (schreiben): attribute (to someone, dative): a quotation, a work, a quality; sich etwas selbst zuzuschreiben haben = have only yourself to blame. Clue: to attribute (a quotation, a work) to someone. Helper: hat zugeschrieben.
